About Me

I am a scientific instrumentation professional with hands-on experience in the installation, troubleshooting, maintenance, and technical support of isotope ratio mass spectrometry systems, inlet systems, elemental analyzers, and associated laboratory instrumentation. In my current role supporting research instrumentation within the Department of Earth, Environmental & Planetary Sciences (DEEPS) at Brown University, I focus on system reliability, analytical performance, and practical scientific problem-solving. My interests extend beyond maintaining systems to understanding, improving, and thinking ahead about the future of scientific instrumentation.
